  • 🌑 Dark Room Search: Move mouse or drag your finger to shine a spotlight over scattered vocabulary or sentence clues.

  • 📸 Flexible Images: Upload files, drag & drop, paste with Ctrl+V / iPad paste, or use image URLs.
